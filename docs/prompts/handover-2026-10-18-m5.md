## Decisions I Made Without Asking

- The rotation lock applies when the hero spawns, so a cancelled or lost pick does not count.
- An ability resolves in the tick of the cast. Ranged has no projectile and no knockback.
- The area ability also hits enemy buildings and the Core, and never blockers or own buildings.
- A stunned hero cannot cast, and a cooldown counts the tick of the cast.
- Shield and melee need no second tap in the UI: melee strikes the current target, shield covers the hero.
- The lux allocation is a share of the Core (25, 50, 75 percent or the most the Core can spare), not a free number, to keep the panel simple on a phone.
- The web `play.e2e.js` test must not tap a tile under the panel: a tap on the barracks tile once landed on the Order button.
