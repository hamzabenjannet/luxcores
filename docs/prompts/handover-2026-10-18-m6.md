# Handover: M6 (Items)

Describes the state after the commits `035d8a0` (sim), `c12bddb` (server) and `618979e` (clients, with GDD 8.4 and the HANDOVER section) plus the documentation commit that follows. `docs/HANDOVER.md` holds the live status, and GDD 8.4 the rules.

## What Changed

- **Interface rule.** A placed building frees the palette selection in both clients; a refused tap keeps it. The Godot live test follows the owners' 3D scene.
- **GDD 7.7.** Hamza confirmed its four open choices.
- **Sim.** Shop, inventory (a count per item id), the four effects, Energy Grab funnel, loot bags, creep drops, map spawns and pickup. The hash gains the item fields and each player's budget and inventory.
- **Server and clients.** The snapshot carries the item fields. Both clients show an item bar, a shop row, queue lines, labels and gems, and send the hero to a tapped item.
- **Tests and replays.** `test/items.test.js` (20 tests), a socket test, a browser test, a Godot live part, parity cases. All goldens were re-recorded, and `items-seed-616161` is new.
