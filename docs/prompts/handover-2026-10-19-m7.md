# Handover: Decisions, Entropy Option, Resolution and M7

Describes the state after the commits listed in `progress.md` under 2026-10-19 and the documentation commit that follows. `docs/HANDOVER.md` holds the live status and the M7 section, and the GDD the rules.

## What Changed

- **Item rules.** Hamza confirmed GDD 8.4. A stun blocks item use. Inventories and the Energy Grab budget reach only the owner's team, through a `match:private` event over Socket.IO, the plain WebSocket and the Redis relay.
- **Test ticks.** Tools and tests stop at 5 000 ticks. The game's limit stays 10 000.
- **Entropy rate.** A per-match option from the host, kept by the replay, the log and the match record, applied to the sim, the bots, resumes and replay verification. Menu field in both clients, lobby line for the guest.
- **Resolution.** The web canvas follows the window at the full pixel ratio on a desktop (cap 2 on touch). The Godot window opens maximized and stretches, and its size inputs are gone.
- **M7.** Six end-of-match counters per team (hashed, in the match result) with a table in both clients, one label per creep group, instanced creeps, shots and blockers and interpolated positions in the web client, and a Follow button for the camera in both clients.

## Verified

See the validation row in `docs/HANDOVER.md`: unit, backend and browser suites pass, the soak passes, and Chromium equals Node.

## Decisions I Made Without Asking

- The entropy range is 0 to 100 percent per minute, in whole percent. It is also the validation range of `POST /matches`.
- Follow is off at the start, a button turns it on, and dragging the map turns it off. Godot follows with an easing of 8 per second, the web client likewise.
- The counters appear in the match result only, never during a match, so a player cannot read the enemy's spending.
- Energy Grab funneling also counts as drained lux, and entropy and the meltdown never count as drained.
- The web client draws units two ticks (100 ms) behind the newest snapshot. A jump of more than 6 tiles in one snapshot is drawn as a teleport.
- The creeps browser test sends its four orders in one call, because a slow page let the queue drain between calls.
